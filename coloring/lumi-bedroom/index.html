Lumi's cozy evening

Drawing in her star pajamas before bed. Lumi is gentle, curious and kind. A free coloring page for kids ages three and up, from the animated series Lumi and the Magical World.

Open the PDF to print All coloring pages

How to print

  1. Open the PDF and choose Print.
  2. Letter size, portrait or landscape as shown. "Fit to page" keeps the margins right.
  3. Plain paper works. Card stock makes it a keepsake.
