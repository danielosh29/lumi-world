Privacy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Who this site is for

This website is written for parents and guardians. It is a companion to the Lumi and the Magical World YouTube channel. It is not designed to collect anything from children, and no part of it asks a child to type, upload or sign up for anything.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, logins or profiles.
  • No comments or messaging.
  • No advertising networks, no ad tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • Coloring pages are plain PDF files you can open and print without giving any information.

Visitor statistics

To understand how many people visit and which pages are useful, the site may use a privacy-focused, cookie-free measurement tool that counts visits in aggregate. It does not identify individual visitors, does not use cookies, and does not follow you to other sites.

Parents' mailing list

Only an adult parent or guardian may join. We ask for your email address and your confirmation that you are eighteen or older. We use it only to tell you about new episodes and coloring pages. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to delete your address at any time.

Links to YouTube

Episodes play on YouTube, which has its own privacy policy and its own settings for families, including YouTube Kids and supervised accounts.

Contact

Questions about privacy or this site: hello@lumimagicalworld.com

Accessibility

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Our commitment

We want every parent and child to be able to use this site. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, and we review the site against them as it grows.

What we have done

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a "skip to content" link.
  • Images carry text alternatives; decorative pictures and background videos are hidden from screen readers.
  • Headings, landmarks and buttons are labeled so screen readers can navigate the page and announce what each control does.
  • Text colors meet contrast guidelines on both light and dark backgrounds.
  • Nothing flashes, nothing plays sound on its own, and animation is switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and large text settings without horizontal scrolling.

Known limitations

  • Coloring pages and party printables are image-based PDF files made for printing. They have no text alternative beyond the description shown on the site.
  • Episodes and Shorts play on YouTube, whose player and captions are controlled by YouTube.

Tell us

If anything on this site is hard for you to use, please write to hello@lumimagicalworld.com and tell us what happened and which device and browser you used. We will do our best to fix it, and to send you the content in another form in the meantime.
