What the series is about

Everyday moments, magical lessons

  • One clear idea per episode: being brave, sharing, kindness, curiosity.
  • Recurring characters kids grow with: Lumi, Pip, Reign and Tilly.
  • Original stories, music and visuals, made with care for ages four to nine.
  • Coloring pages match the episodes, so the story continues off-screen.

Safe by design

  • This site collects nothing from children: no accounts, no comments, no tracking ads.
  • Coloring pages are plain PDF files, free, no sign-up needed.
